The problem

The easy deposits are gone. The data isn't.

For over a century geologists searched regions like the Zambian Copperbelt by eye. The remaining giant orebodies are deeper, hidden beneath cover, invisible at surface and buried inside enormous, noisy datasets. The limiting factor was never the rock — it was the inability to connect the clues.

Buried & blind

Targets sit beneath Kalahari sand, laterite and Miombo canopy where copper-only campaigns never looked for arsenic-gold or gem pathfinders.

Siloed datasets

Magnetics, gravity, radiometrics, geochem and imagery were each read in isolation. The signal lives in their coincidence.

Archive lock-in

Decades of insight sat in paper maps, colonial reports and drill logs. We digitise and reason over all of it.
